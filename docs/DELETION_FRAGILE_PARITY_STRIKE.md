# Deletion-fragile parity and exceptional-S6 strike

## Binding status

```text
GRC CE: NO
```

No pair of nonisomorphic finite simple graphs with equal full vertex decks is
claimed here.

## The proposed hole

The covering-space search showed that ordinary voltage scaling preserves too
much gauge symmetry.  The sharper idea was a deletion-fragile CFI lock:

```text
intact parent:       odd/even twist is globally rigid
one vertex deleted: an odd local switch becomes available
```

If every deletion released such a switch, the even and odd parents would have
equal full decks while remaining nonisomorphic.  This is the right logical
shape for a counterexample, rather than merely a source of high deck overlap.

## Exact translation obstruction

Let `C` be a subset of `F_2^d`.  A word `c in C` has an odd-translation
release if some odd vector `x` satisfies

```text
C symmetric_difference (C+x) = {c,c+x}, with c+x outside C.
```

Equivalently, translation by `x` preserves `C-{c}` and only the deleted word
prevented it from being an odd symmetry of the intact gadget.

**Theorem.** If every word of `C` has an odd-translation release, then `C` is
a singleton.

Proof.  A witness `x` gives a perfect matching of the cube with exactly one
cut pair, incident with `c`.  For two witnesses `x,y`, the two unique cut
edges occur in one `x,y` four-cycle.  The parity of a cut around a four-cycle
is even, so those cut edges meet.  Since their distinct endpoints in `C` are
both inside, they share their outside endpoint.  Hence all witness edges have
one common outside endpoint `q`, and every `c in C` has parity opposite to
`q`.  But an odd witness at `c` must pair every word of `C-{c}` with another
word of `C-{c}` across parity.  All those words have the same parity, so
`C-{c}` is empty.  Therefore `|C|=1`.  QED.

This rules out the naive CFI repair in every dimension, not merely those that
were computed.

## Full cube-isometry classification

The next relaxation permits coordinate relabeling as well as an odd switch.
For every nontrivial subset `C` and every odd cube isometry `g`, the exact
test asks whether

```text
g(C-{c}) = C-{d}
```

for a source word `c` and some target word `d`, while no odd isometry
stabilizes intact `C`.  Exhaustive results are:

| dimension | subsets checked | odd isometries | complete release codes |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 10 | 4 | 0 |
| 3 | 246 | 24 | 0 |
| 4 | 65,518 | 192 | 0 |

Thus neither the standard CFI middle code nor any nontrivial relabeled cube
code through dimension four has the required deletion behavior.

## New pivot: the exceptional outer automorphism of S6

The failure identifies what a successful gadget needs: deletion must unlock
an isomorphism not generated by the intact gadget's ordinary switch group.
The exceptional outer automorphism of `S6` supplies exactly two inequivalent
notions of a deleted point.

The implementation constructs it without a lookup table:

- 15 duads (pairs of six points);
- 15 synthemes (partitions into three duads);
- six totals (partitions of all duads into five synthemes);
- the faithful action of `S6` on the six totals.

Exact certificates:

| property | result |
|---|---:|
| permutations in the outer image | 720 |
| ordinary point stabilizer | 120 |
| exotic total stabilizer | 120 |
| ordinary stabilizer orbits on six points | `1+5` |
| exotic stabilizer orbits on six points | `6` |
| every point/total stabilizer intersection | 20 |

The orbit profiles prove that the two `S5` subgroups are not conjugate in
`S6`.  The outer action also exchanges transpositions with triple
transpositions, 3-cycles with pairs of 3-cycles, and 6-cycles with elements
of type `3+2+1`.

This is the new construction primitive: couple an ordinary six-point action
to the exotic six-total action so that a card may use a point/total
stabilizer intersection, but a parent isomorphism would have to implement the
outer automorphism by conjugation, which is impossible.

## What is and is not closed

Closed:

- pure odd-translation parity release in every dimension;
- all nontrivial full cube-isometry release codes through dimension four;
- the standard CFI local-code repair at its intended algebraic level.

Open and now sharply specified:

- a mixed ordinary/exotic `S6` incidence structure whose 15 deletion cards
  admit the required local outer identifications;
- binary realization with exact full-deck equality;
- legal-order parent nonisomorphism and hostile independent verification.

The natural carrier is the 15-element duad/syntheme geometry, already above
the verified `n<=13` boundary.  The next search is over unions of exact
`S6` orbitals and local stabilizer double cosets, not over generic graphs.
