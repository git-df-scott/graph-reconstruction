# Exceptional-S6 deletion-adapted direct strike

## Binding verdict

```text
GRC CE: NO
```

No nonisomorphic finite simple graphs with equal full unlabeled vertex decks
were found.  The direct exceptional-`S6` architecture is now closed exactly.

## Frozen exceptional geometry

The construction begins with six points, their 15 duads, the 15 synthemes,
and the six totals.  The action on totals gives the exceptional outer
automorphism.  It is normalized by the inner permutation

```text
(2 4)(3 5)
```

in zero-based notation so that the resulting outer automorphism `beta` is an
involution.  All `720^2 = 518,400` multiplication pairs were replayed:

```text
beta(gh) = beta(g) beta(h)
beta(beta(g)) = g
|image(beta)| = 720.
```

The ordinary point stabilizer and exotic total stabilizer both have order
120.  They are nonconjugate: the former has point-orbit structure `1+5`,
while the latter is transitive.  Exhaustive conjugation is the independent
nonconjugacy check.

## Direct 15-carrier classification

The action of `S6` on the 15 duads has only two orbitals on unordered pairs:

| relation | edges |
|---|---:|
| intersecting duads | 60 |
| disjoint duads | 45 |

Consequently there are only four invariant simple graphs: empty, the two
complementary orbital graphs, and complete.  Outer twisting does not even
change the permutation subgroup on this carrier, because `beta(S6)=S6`.

More generally, every graph obtained as a union of orbitals of a transitive
action is regular.  Regular graphs are reconstructible from one card: the
deck reconstructs the valency `r`, and in `G-v` the former neighbors of `v`
are exactly the vertices of degree `r-1`; the remaining vertices have degree
`r`.  Adding the missing vertex is therefore unique.

This closes all transitive orbital graphs, not only the four 15-vertex
controls.

## Semilinear rescue theorem

Let `alpha` be an automorphism of a finite group `Gamma`.  For every subgroup
`H`, the map

```text
gH -> alpha(g) alpha(H)
```

is a well-defined ordinary bijection of coset carriers.  It carries every
`Gamma`-invariant relation to its functorial `alpha`-twist.

Therefore nonconjugacy of `H` and `alpha(H)` inside `Gamma` does **not** imply
nonisomorphism of the resulting uncolored graphs.  The semilinear coset map
is an unrestricted graph isomorphism outside the intended `Gamma` action.
This is the precise flaw in the original direct outer-twist hope.

## Complete minimal nontransitive classification

To permit irregular graphs, the four natural carriers were combined:

```text
P + T + D + S
6 + 6 + 15 + 15 = 42 vertices,
```

where `P,D` carry the ordinary action and `T,S` the exceptional action.  The
diagonal `S6` action has exactly 15 orbitals on the 861 unordered vertex
pairs.  Hence the complete invariant binary-relation domain has only

```text
2^15 = 32,768
```

graphs.  This is an orbital classification, not generic graph enumeration.

For each graph only four representative cards are needed, with exact
multiplicities `6,6,15,15`.  Stable equitable quotients were used only as a
safe rejection invariant.  Every pair surviving that invariant was replayed
against literal vertex permutations.

Exact results:

| quantity | count |
|---|---:|
| raw invariant graphs | 32,768 |
| stable deck-quotient classes | 14,912 |
| singleton classes | 384 |
| classes of size 2 | 12,864 |
| classes of size 4 | 1,664 |
| parent-isomorphic deck-collision pairs | 22,848 |
| nonisomorphic deck collisions | **0** |

Every one of the 22,848 collision pairs has an explicit parent isomorphism:

| rescue permutation | pairs |
|---|---:|
| swap only the 15-carriers | 3,840 |
| swap only the 6-carriers | 3,520 |
| full semilinear swap | 15,488 |

The logic is exact.  Different stable card quotients imply unequal ordinary
decks.  Equal quotients always trigger a raw adjacency-preserving parent
permutation.  Thus there are no unresolved pairs hidden behind the quotient
invariant.

## Complete S6 subgroup / Gassmann classification

All conjugacy classes of subgroups of `S6` were generated by adjoining every
possible group element, closing the generated subgroup, and canonicalizing
under all 720 conjugations.  The generator-chain construction is complete:
every subgroup is reached along one of its generating chains.

Results:

```text
subgroup conjugacy classes:                    56
outer-nonconjugate directed classes:           38
outer-balanced nonconjugate directed classes:   2
unordered outer-balanced pairs:                 1
```

The unique pair consists of two Klein four groups:

```text
H = <(0 1)(2 3), (0 2)(1 3)>
K = <(0 1)(2 3), (0 1)(4 5)>.
```

Each contains the identity and three double transpositions, so `H,K` are
almost conjugate (their intersections with every `S6` conjugacy class have
the same size).  They are not conjugate: `H` has two common fixed points and
`K` has none.  The outer automorphism exchanges their conjugacy classes.

Their coset degree is

```text
[S6:H] = [S6:K] = 180.
```

This is a genuine Gassmann/Sunada primitive, but its direct orbital graphs
are transitive and hence regular, so it cannot itself produce a GRC
counterexample.

## Strongest surviving primitive and next strike

The unique `V4` Gassmann pair is the strongest survivor.  The next justified
carrier is

```text
S6/H + ordinary six-point anchor + exotic six-point anchor
```

against the corresponding `S6/K` carrier, of order `180+6+6=192`.

The required move is a **degree-balanced partial twist**: the anchor coupling
must break the full semilinear swap without making the two anchor types
reconstructible from degrees or exposing them after internal deletion.  The
search variables are cross-orbital/double-coset choices, not arbitrary graph
edges.  Full twisting is forbidden by the semilinear-rescue theorem, and a
single transitive coset carrier is forbidden by regular reconstruction.
