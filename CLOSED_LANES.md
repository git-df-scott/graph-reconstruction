# Closed-lane audit

The word “closed” is allowed only with an explicit qualifier below.

## Theorem-closed mechanisms

| Mechanism | Exact closure | What remains outside it |
|---|---|---|
| Exact-label globalizer | If one parent permutation preserves every shared edge-class label, every binary realization is parent-isomorphic. | Zero-globalizer systems and assignment-specific isomorphisms. |
| Pure odd-translation CFI release | A binary code for which deletion of every word unlocks an odd translation is a singleton. | General cube isometries above dimension 4; non-CFI synchronizers. |
| Transitive orbital graphs | Every orbital union of a transitive group action is regular; a regular graph is reconstructible from one card. | Nontransitive and non-invariant group constructions. |
| Functorial outer twist | `gH -> alpha(g)alpha(H)` is an unrestricted semilinear isomorphism between functorially twisted coset relations. | Partial/nonfunctorial twists designed to break that map. |
| Fully invariant rigid-anchor row family | Equality after one row deletion restores the invariant row multiplicity function. | Non-invariant trades whose symmetry appears only after deletion. |
| Large-cell vector decks | Under the stated twin-free quotient assumptions, blow-ups with every non-singleton class at least 3 are reconstructed by the weighted vector deck. | Mixed singleton/size-2 cells and changing quotient structure. |
| Two separated degrees | A graph whose two degree values differ by at least 2 is reconstructed from one card. | Consecutive two-degree graphs. |
| One-card fibre reduction | Every CE is a pair of one-vertex extensions of a shared card. | Finding a card whose extension fibre actually collides. |
| Odd complement-deck fixed card | If an odd-order deck is invariant under complementation, some card is self-complementary. | Even order, and the still-incomplete order-17 fibre campaign. |

The order-22 two-root repair over the one specified 20-vertex selector core
has a separately verified UNSAT proof: 82 edge bits, 33,201 SAT variables,
1,595,879 clauses, and 175,104 explicitly excluded parent maps. DRAT-trim
verified the proof and an independent VF2 implementation reproduced the full
parent-map set. See `ASTRA_DIRECT_CE_STRIKE.md`; do not generalize this finite
closure to other two-root cores.
The three-root repair at the same parent order is not closed and was not
searched in this session.

## Heuristically dead, not closed

- Random near-identity `7x7` incidence systems: 110,000 samples.
- The unsupported Blue-LED pair/triple/four-way joins and 50,000 asymmetric
  run: not even reproducible from the repository.
- Hong-regime annealed samples at orders 14 and 15.
- Sampled automorphism-cycle-type graphs at order 14.
- Sampled quotient-8 blow-ups and order-15 blow-up cards.
- Random-start and structured-seed deck-overlap climbs.
- Sampled order-18 and order-20 bi-circulants.
- Incomplete complement-deck SAT instances and Delta instances above the
  explicitly completed small shapes.

Search failure in these lanes is only guidance about proposal quality.

## Explicitly open or unresolved lanes

### Pseudosimilarity

Open.  The campaign has one exact asymmetric base family and small census
controls.  It did not classify pseudosimilar cores, mutually pseudosimilar
sets, or extension-neighborhood cocycles in general.

### Voltage and monodromy covers

Open.  Ordinary voltage scaling over one base repeatedly produced quotient
duplicates.  Locally conjugate but globally nonconjugate representations with
deletion-dependent conjugators were not constructed or exhausted.

### Gassmann / Sunada

Open.  Direct invariant `S6` uses are blocked, but non-invariant partial
couplings, other groups, and deletion-dependent anchors remain untouched.

### Incidence and design trades

Open.  Induced hypergraph hypomorphy failed to survive trace deletion.
Trace-hypomorphic set systems or paired row/column trades were not classified.

### Stockmeyer-style synchronization

Open in graph-native form.  The sign obstruction disappears under symmetric
pair rules, while rigid multi-vertex encodings lose whole-vertex deletion.
A deletion-fragile synchronizer remains hypothetical.

### One-card fibres

Open and methodologically important.  The reduction is valid, but the current
implementation's 64-bit deduplication and parent-equality decisions prevent
silent runs from being certified as exact.  The self-complementary `4^4`
order-16 card family, 18-edge sparse family, and several cycle types were also
unfinished.

### Seven order-20 bi-circulants

Unresolved timeouts.  They are neither counterexample candidates nor negative
results.
